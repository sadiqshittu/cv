\section{Summary}

I am a PhD candidate whose research follows a full empirical loop. First, I build distributed infrastructure to audit and measure how security tools are deployed at scale. Next, I conduct user studies and interviews to evaluate how people use those tools, identifying what works, diagnosing workflow and mental-model failures, and pinpointing areas for improvement. Then, I apply these findings to build improved prototypes of those tools, then evaluate and iteratively refine them. This places me among a small group of researchers who combine systems security and engineering, empirical measurement, and human-subjects research, three areas that are traditionally studied separately.
